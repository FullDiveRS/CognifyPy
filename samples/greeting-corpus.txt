# greeting-corpus.txt — teach a Cognify mind conversation basics.
#
# The demo corpus (corpus-part*.txt) is a DICTIONARY: every line is
# "Word means ...", and it contains no greetings at all — so a mind trained
# only on it has never seen "hello" and answers with word salad. Feed this
# file too, then deep-train, and greetings become speakable:
#
#     mind.train_file("greeting-corpus.txt")
#     mind.train_deep_model(TransformerTrainOptions(epochs=12, decay=0.9))
#
# Plain sentences, one per line; blank lines and '#' comments are ignored.
# Words repeat on purpose — a tiny word-level model learns by repetition.
Hello is a warm word people say when they meet.
When someone says hello, you say hello back.
Hello means I am glad to see you.
Say hello to greet a friend.
Hello friend, welcome here.
Hello is how a talk begins.
Hi is a short way to say hello.
Hi means hello.
When someone says hi, you say hi back.
Good morning is a hello for the start of the day.
Good evening is a hello for the end of the day.
A greeting is a hello or a hi.
Greet a friend with hello.
Welcome means I am glad you came.
A welcome is what you say when someone arrives.
Welcome friend, come in.
When someone says hello, a kind answer is hello to you too.
Talking starts when you say hello.
Saying hello is friendly and polite.
Hello there, it is good to see you.
It is good to see you means welcome.
Thank you is what you say when someone is kind.
Please is a polite word when you ask for something.
Sorry is what you say when you did something wrong.
Goodbye is what you say when you leave.
Goodbye means the talk is over.
When someone says goodbye, you say goodbye back.
See you later is a friendly goodbye.
A friend is someone you like to talk with.
Be kind when you talk.
Be polite when you talk.
A question is how you ask about something.
An answer is what you say to a question.
When someone asks a question, you try to answer.
Listening means you pay attention to words.
Pay attention when someone talks to you.
A name is what people call you.
My name tells who I am.
I am glad when a friend says hello.
Saying welcome makes a friend feel good.
Talking with friends feels warm.
A talk grows when you listen and answer.

# --- "how are you" exchanges -------------------------------------------
# Without these lines a mind asked "how are you" has never seen the
# phrase, so it recombines corpus words at random (word salad).
How are you is a question a friend asks.
How are you means I want to know how you feel.
When someone asks how are you, you say I am doing well.
When someone asks how are you, a kind answer is I am doing well.
I am doing well means I feel good today.
I am fine means I am doing well.
How are you today?
I am doing well today, thank you for asking.
A friend asks how are you when they meet you.
How are you is a hello that asks about feelings.
When someone asks how are you, be kind and answer.
An answer to how are you is I am well, thank you.
You ask how are you, and the answer is I am doing well.
When a friend says how are you, it is a kind hello.
